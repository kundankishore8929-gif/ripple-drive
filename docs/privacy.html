Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Ripple Drive is a free, open-source Mac app that shows your Google Drive as a disk on your Mac. It runs entirely on your Mac. There is no Ripple Drive server, account, analytics or advertising.

What Ripple Drive accesses

With your permission, Ripple Drive uses the Google Drive API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. It needs this to list, open, upload, search and share the files in your Google Drive (My Drive and Shared with me), because it shows your whole Drive as a disk.

How your data is used

  • Your files and file details travel only between your Mac and Google's servers, and only when you or an app on your Mac opens, saves, uploads, searches or shares them.
  • Parts of files you open are kept in a temporary cache on your Mac (~/Library/Caches/RippleDrive), capped at 2 GB and cleared 15 minutes after use.
  • Your Google sign-in token and client secret are stored only on your Mac, in ~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf, readable only by your macOS user.
  • Upload progress records and logs stay on your Mac (~/.config/ripple-drive, ~/Library/Logs/RippleDrive).

What is never done

  • No data from Google is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No data is collected for analytics or tracking.

Ripple Drive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Run zsh uninstall.sh --all to delete Ripple Drive and its sign-in from your Mac. Revoke its access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
